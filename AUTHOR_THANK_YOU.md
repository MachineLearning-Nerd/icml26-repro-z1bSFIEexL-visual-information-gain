# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Seulbi Lee and Sangheum Hwang for introducing Visual Information Gain as a clear sample- and token-level way to discuss how much a vision-language model uses visual evidence. The paper’s explicit equations, selective-training protocol, and benchmark descriptions made it possible to pin the source and build a narrowly scoped formula audit.

This repository is intended to credit the original work while keeping a strict boundary between the authors’ reported LVLM results, the pinned paper artifacts, and the local deterministic toy. It does not claim author endorsement or constitute an official implementation.
